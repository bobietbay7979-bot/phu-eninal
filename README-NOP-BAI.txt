CACH NOP BAI

1. Giai nen phu-yen-submission.zip.
2. Dua index.html, css/, js/, assets/ va responsive-demo.html len thu muc goc cua repository GitHub. Giu nguyen ten va cau truc cac thu muc.
3. Trong GitHub: Settings > Pages > Deploy from a branch; chon nhanh chua ma (thuong la main) va /(root), sau do Save.
4. Mo dia chi website do GitHub Pages hien thi va kiem tra trang da len. Dan chinh dia chi GitHub Pages do vao o website link cua bai nop.
5. Tai project-evidence.pdf len o tep dinh kem. PDF da co mo ta du an, bang chung W3C, responsive, CSS va bon tinh nang bo sung. Khong bat buoc quay video vi de cho phep mo ta bang van ban.
6. Dinh kem css/style.css de nguoi cham xem toan bo CSS. Neu he thong chi nhan mot tep, nop goi ZIP nay; PDF va anh bang chung nam trong submission/.
7. Neu can dan mo ta vao o van ban, dung submission/project-description.txt.

Luu y: Chua co dia chi GitHub Pages moi vi ban se tu upload. Khong dung duong dan tai tep trong cuoc tro chuyen lam link website. URL xuat hien trong anh W3C la ban demo da duoc kiem tra; ma HTML va CSS trong goi nay trung khop ban do.

TEP CAN BIET
- index.html, css/style.css, js/script.js, assets/: ma nguon website.
- responsive-demo.html: demo cung mot trang trong hai khung nhin.
- submission/project-evidence.pdf: tai lieu mo ta va bang chung de nop.
- submission/project-description.txt: mo ta bang tieng Anh de dan vao bai.
- submission/evidence/: anh goc, phan hoi W3C va thong tin kiem tra.
